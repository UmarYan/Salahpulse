SalahPulse v7

Private, offline-first Salah tracker.

Highlights:
- Daily 5-Salah tracking with popup-first entry
- Separate Adherence, Timeliness and Jama'ah metrics
- Per-Salah consistency insights
- Recent-weighted Consistency Strength
- Qaza kept separate from today's five prayers
- Clear/not-recorded is excluded from adherence calculations
- Prayer-specific Usual Jama'ah time and Personal on-time target settings
- Clear explanations for On time, Delayed, Qaza, Missed and Clear
- Reflection, streaks, history, matrix, backup/restore and CSV export
- Day/Night/Auto theme
- Offline-first local storage

Important timing note:
Usual Jama'ah time is the normal congregation time and is not the prayer start time.
Personal on-time target is an app tracking target, not a Shariah definition.
Actual prayer start/end times vary by date and location.
